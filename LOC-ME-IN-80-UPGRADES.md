4. **Accent Color Studio** — previews and remembers five color directions with accessory pairing tips.
5. **My Lookbook** — saves portfolio favorites locally and exposes a Favorites-only view.
6. **Appointment Kit** — packages the chosen style, date, time, and estimate for copying or calendar download.
