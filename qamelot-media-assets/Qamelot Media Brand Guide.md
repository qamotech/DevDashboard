# Qamelot Media Brand Guide

> Qamelot Media is a fictional creative brand. All names, services, products, claims, and campaign materials in this kit are conceptual.

## Brand premise

Qamelot Media is a fictional digital studio that turns business problems into polished web apps. It presents software development as a creative quest: clients bring the challenge, and Qamelot equips them with a fast, practical digital tool.

Qamelot does not promise magic. It makes complicated technology feel magical through thoughtful design, clear communication, and reliable execution.

## Brand story

Beyond the ordinary internet lies Qamelot—a bright digital kingdom where tangled workflows become useful tools.

Qamelot Media began with a simple belief: a web app should feel less like a software project and more like gaining a new superpower. Its fictional guild of designers, developers, and pixel-sized creatures creates focused applications for businesses, creators, communities, and ambitious teams.

Every project begins as a quest. Every obstacle becomes a feature. Every launch adds something useful to the kingdom.

## Audience

- Small businesses replacing spreadsheets and manual workflows
- Creators launching interactive products or communities
- Startups testing focused web-app ideas
- Teams needing dashboards, portals, calculators, or internal tools
- Organizations that want custom software without corporate complexity

They value speed, personality, transparency, mobile usability, and solutions that solve real problems.

## Personality

- Inventive, but practical
- Playful, but dependable
- Bold, but approachable
- Technically capable, but never intimidating
- Slightly theatrical, but always clear
- Optimistic, curious, and quest-driven

The voice uses short sentences, clever fantasy-tech language, and confident calls to action. Puns are occasional seasoning—not the entire meal.

## Slogan

Supporting lines:

## Color palette

| Color | Hex | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Midnight Keep | `#11152B` | Primary background and authority |
| Quest Violet | `#6957FF` | Core brand color and interactive elements |
| Mana Mint | `#78F7C1` | Success, highlights, and energetic accents |
| Crown Gold | `#FFD45A` | Premium details and calls to attention |
| Dragon Coral | `#FF6F61` | Warm actions, personality, and alerts |
| Cloud Parchment | `#F7F3E8` | Light backgrounds and editorial surfaces |
| Crystal White | `#FFFFFF` | Contrast and clean interface space |

Gradients should feel like controlled digital light rather than generic neon effects. The preferred combination is Quest Violet fading into Mana Mint over Midnight Keep.

## Typography mood

Headlines feel monumental, compact, and slightly fantastical—like modern titles carved into a digital castle. Body typography is geometric, welcoming, and highly readable.

Recommended direction:

- Display: a bold, high-contrast serif with dramatic shapes
- Interface headings: a wide or geometric grotesk
- Body: a clean humanist sans serif
- Technical details: a restrained monospaced face

Suggested pairing: **Fraunces** for expressive display copy, **Space Grotesk** for navigation and body content, and **IBM Plex Mono** for technical labels.

## Logo direction

The primary logo combines a stylized uppercase **Q** with a simplified castle portal.

The Q’s circular form becomes a glowing gateway. Its tail transforms into a cursor arrow or winding quest path. Three small square battlements reference both castle architecture and digital pixels.

Logo variants:

- Full wordmark: Q symbol with “Qamelot Media”
- Compact mark: portal-shaped Q
- App icon: Q gateway on Midnight Keep
- Monochrome seal: simplified Q inside a circular guild badge

Avoid detailed shields, swords, medieval calligraphy, or realistic heraldry. The identity should feel like a digital product company with a playful fantasy layer.

## Mascot

### Quillby the Pixel Wyvern

Quillby is a small violet-and-mint digital dragon who lives inside unfinished web apps. Instead of breathing fire, Quillby exhales glowing interface components, charts, buttons, and tiny lines of code.

Character traits:

- Curious and energetic
- Carries a miniature golden cursor
- Has square, pixel-like wing tips
- Celebrates successful launches by producing three mint-colored sparks
- Appears in onboarding, loading states, empty states, tutorials, and launch announcements

Quillby should remain simple enough to read at favicon size while supporting expressive poses in larger illustrations.

## Packaging

Because Qamelot sells digital products, its packaging is an interactive delivery experience called a **Quest Kit**.

Each completed web app includes:

- A digital treasure-card cover featuring the app name
- A “Quest Complete” launch certificate
- A branded project portal
- App access and administrator links
- A visual quick-start guide
- A short walkthrough video
- A maintenance and support map
- A digital “Forged in Qamelot” badge
- Optional social launch graphics

The visual packaging uses dark presentation panels, gold dividers, mint status lights, oversized app screenshots, and Quillby guiding the client through each item.

## Fifteen-second launch video

**Format:** 15 seconds, adaptable to 16:9 and 9:16
**Mood:** Cinematic, playful, fast, confident

WEB APPS WORTHY OF YOUR NEXT QUEST.

**Call to action:** START YOUR QUEST

## Three-note sonic signature

**Notes:** C4 → G4 → D5
**Rhythm:** Short · short · sustained
**Total duration:** Approximately 1.4 seconds

1. **C4:** A warm wooden pluck representing the start of a quest
2. **G4:** A bright digital bell representing the app taking shape
3. **D5:** A sustained glass-and-mint shimmer representing successful launch

The final note ends with three quiet sparkling particles—Quillby’s audio signature.

## Coherence rules

- Every asset uses the same seven-color palette.
- The Q-shaped digital portal is the primary visual motif.
- Quillby appears as a guide, never as visual clutter.
- Fantasy references remain playful and abstract.
- Product interfaces always look modern and useful.
- Copy emphasizes practical outcomes before fantasy language.
- No asset suggests that Qamelot Media is a real operating company.
- Avoid real customer names, fabricated testimonials, performance claims, or invented awards.
