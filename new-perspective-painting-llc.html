Painting FAQ

Useful answers before the first coat.

Project details vary by surface and condition, but these are the decisions that usually shape a successful painting project.

What painting services are available?

Interior painting, exterior painting, cabinet refinishing, feature walls and murals, commercial painting, preparation and minor surface repair, plus color and finish consultation.

How do I prepare for an estimate?

List the rooms or exterior areas, the surfaces involved, known repairs, approximate dimensions, your preferred timing, and any color ideas. Photos can also help explain the scope.

Can you help me choose colors and sheen?

Yes. Color, lighting, surrounding materials, surface type, traffic, moisture, cleaning needs, and the desired mood should all inform the choice. Semi-gloss is recommended for medium- and high-traffic kitchens and bathrooms.

What affects the price of painting?

Surface area, preparation and repair needs, access, number of colors, coating system, trim and detail work, occupied-space protection, and schedule all affect the final scope.

Why are physical paint samples still important?

Screens display color differently. A sample lets you judge the real coating against the room’s daylight, evening light, flooring, furniture, and fixed materials.
