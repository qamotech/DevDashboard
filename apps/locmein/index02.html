✦ Natural Loc Care Protocols

  • Moisturize daily with a light organic rosewater and essential oil spray.
  • Always protect your locs at night with a satin scarf or silk bonnet.
  • Ensure locs are completely dry after shampoo sessions to prevent moisture retention.
  • Schedule retwists every 4-6 weeks to maintain clean partings and root health.
