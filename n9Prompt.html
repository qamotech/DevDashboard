Step 1 · Define the Task Goal

State the outcome you want in ONE clear sentence. Strong prompts start with a strong goal.

💡 Tip: Use an action verb (Summarize, Draft, Extract, Translate, Classify, Refactor…).
🧭

Step 2 · Add Context

Relevant background, data sources, audience, and situational information the AI needs to know.

🛡️

Step 3 · Set Constraints

Tone, format rules, length limits, and safety guardrails.

📋

Step 4 · Provide Instructions

Ordered, atomic steps for the AI to follow. Use the hierarchy to signal priority.

🧪

Step 5 · Include Examples

Few-shot input/output pairs teach the AI the exact style and shape you want.

📤

Step 6 · Specify Output Format

Tell the AI the exact shape of the response.

🔗

Step 7 · Add Variables

Define placeholders like {user_name} for reusable, dynamic prompts.

💡 Reference a variable anywhere in your prompt with curly braces, e.g. {deadline}.
